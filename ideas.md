## Option A: "Brutalist Precision" — Industrial Modernism

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Typographic Style meets Digital Brutalism

**Core Principles:**
1. Stark contrast between raw structure and refined data — heavy black borders, exposed grid lines
2. Typography as architecture — text blocks used as visual elements, not just content
3. Information density with intentional breathing room — dense data panels separated by bold white voids
4. Monochromatic with a single electric accent — no gradients, no softness

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Near-black (#0A0A0A) with off-white (#F5F0E8) panels
- Primary accent: Electric amber (#F5A623) — signals intelligence, urgency, precision
- Secondary: Steel gray (#8A8A8A) for supporting text
- Emotional intent: Authority, technical mastery, no-nonsense expertise

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric column grid — 7:5 ratio splits, not centered
- Sections delineated by thick horizontal rules (4px borders)
- Navigation: Left-rail vertical nav pinned to side, not top bar
- Content blocks stagger left/right like a technical report

**Signature Elements:**
1. Exposed grid overlay — faint dotted grid visible in hero background
2. Monospace data counters — animated stat blocks in Courier-style font
3. Bold section labels in ALL CAPS with tracking — "DISCOVERY / 01"

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states: border color shifts from gray to amber
- Click feedback: brief invert flash (white-on-black)
- No smooth curves — transitions are sharp, 100ms

**Animation:**
- Text enters via clip-path reveal (left to right wipe)
- Numbers count up from zero on scroll entry
- No parallax — elements snap into position

**Typography System:**
- Display: Space Grotesk Bold (700) — geometric, technical
- Body: IBM Plex Mono Regular — reinforces code/logic aesthetic
- Labels: Space Grotesk Medium with wide letter-spacing
- Scale: 72px / 48px / 32px / 18px / 14px

## Option B: "Deep Intelligence" — Dark Aerospace Minimalism ✅ SELECTED

**Design Movement:** Aerospace HMI (Human-Machine Interface) meets Premium SaaS Dark Mode

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep space darkness as a canvas — content emerges from shadow, not sits on white
2. Precision over decoration — every element earns its place through function
3. Luminous accents that suggest neural activity — teal/cyan glow effects
4. Structural asymmetry — content blocks offset to suggest dynamic motion

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep navy-black (#050B18) — depth without pure black harshness
- Surface: Slate blue (#0D1B2E) for cards and panels
- Primary accent: Cyan-teal (#00D4C8) — intelligence, technology, trust
- Secondary accent: Electric blue (#2563EB) — action, links, CTAs
- Text: Crisp white (#F8FAFC) primary, slate (#94A3B8) secondary
- Emotional intent: Cutting-edge expertise, trustworthy precision, forward-thinking

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Diagonal section breaks using clip-path — sections flow into each other at angles
- Hero: Full-bleed left-aligned text with right-side abstract visualization
- Services: Horizontal scroll cards on desktop, stacked on mobile
- Stats: Wide horizontal band with large numbers — breaks the vertical rhythm
- Asymmetric content: Text and visuals never perfectly centered

**Signature Elements:**
1. Glowing circuit-line SVG decorations — thin lines that pulse with CSS animation
2. Frosted glass cards (backdrop-blur) with subtle border glow on hover
3. Gradient text headings — cyan to blue on key phrases

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover: Cards lift with box-shadow glow (cyan tint)
- CTAs: Gradient border button that fills on hover
- Scroll: Staggered fade-up entrance animations via Intersection Observer

**Animation:**
- Hero: Floating particle field in canvas background (subtle, slow)
- Section entrance: Elements slide up 20px and fade in, staggered 100ms per item
- Counters: Number roll-up animation on scroll
- Nav: Blur-in on scroll past hero

**Typography System:**
- Display: Syne ExtraBold (800) — futuristic, distinctive, not Inter
- Subheadings: Syne Bold (700)
- Body: DM Sans Regular (400) — clean, readable, modern
- Monospace accents: JetBrains Mono — for code snippets, stats labels
- Scale: 80px / 56px / 36px / 22px / 16px

## Option C: "Structured Clarity" — Editorial Corporate Modernism

**Design Movement:** Editorial Magazine Layout meets B2B SaaS (Stripe/Linear aesthetic)

**Core Principles:**
1. Light, airy, and authoritative — white space as confidence
2. Strong typographic hierarchy — size and weight do the heavy lifting
3. Warm neutrals with a single bold accent — approachable yet professional
4. Modular editorial grid — content arranged like a premium business publication

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Warm white (#FAFAF8) — not clinical, slightly warm
- Surface: Light stone (#F0EDE8) for alternate sections
- Primary accent: Forest green (#1B6B3A) — growth, trust, sustainability
- Secondary: Warm charcoal (#2D2D2D) for headings
- Emotional intent: Trustworthy partner, growth-focused, accessible expertise

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Magazine-style editorial grid — 3-column with varying content widths
- Pull quotes and callout boxes break the text flow
- Alternating image-left / text-right sections
- Large typographic section dividers

**Signature Elements:**
1. Oversized drop caps or large decorative numbers for section markers
2. Thin horizontal rules with category labels
3. Photo-realistic imagery in editorial cutout style

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover: Subtle underline animations on links
- Cards: Gentle scale-up (1.02) with shadow deepening
- Transitions: 300ms ease — smooth and professional

**Animation:**
- Fade-in on scroll — simple, clean, no distractions
- Headline words animate in sequentially
- Progress indicator on long-scroll pages

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display Bold — editorial authority

## Selected Design: Option B — "Deep Intelligence"

Rationale: The aerospace dark mode aesthetic perfectly aligns with AI-CoreLogic's brand positioning as a cutting-edge, precision-focused AI consultancy. The deep navy palette with cyan accents communicates technological sophistication while remaining trustworthy. The diagonal section breaks and asymmetric layout avoid the "generic AI startup" look. Syne + DM Sans typography is distinctive without being illegible.

## Style Decisions

- **Composition rule:** Every major section uses an asymmetric aerospace-HMI structure through offset module labels, directional routing lines, telemetry bands, or diagonal transitions rather than relying on a centered heading and uniform card grid as the primary composition.
- **Signature motif rule:** AI-CoreLogic uses thin cyan circuit/HUD linework, coordinate labels, node paths, mono telemetry details, and restrained status indicators as a recurring brand device.
- **Copy voice rule:** Language should sound like a precise AI implementation advisor—diagnostic, operational, and evidence-led—avoiding generic AI hype unless it is grounded in a concrete business outcome.
- **Accent discipline:** Bright cyan and electric blue are reserved for primary actions, active system states, and decision-critical information; supporting surfaces remain darker and quieter.
